## Line Focus

Highlight and focus on specific lines.

```bash
$ affinity main.py --focus 10-15
[Affinity] Rendering main.py (Lines 10-15)

 10 | def process_data(data):
 11 |     cleaned = sanitize(data)
 12 |     results = []
 13 |     for item in cleaned:
 14 |         results.append(transform(item))
 15 |     return results

[Affinity] Focus mode active.
```
